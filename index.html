vCard carries every field and works on iPhone and Android. MeCard makes a smaller, easier-to-scan code but drops job title and splits less cleanly. Keep dots dark on a light background for reliable scanning.

Logo

A logo hides part of the code, so adding one switches error correction to H. Keep it at 25% or less and test-scan before printing. The image never leaves your browser.
